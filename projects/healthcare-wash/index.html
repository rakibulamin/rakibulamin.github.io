    Planning for sustainable healthcare WASH

    Published: November 05, 2026

    Terre des hommes · Teknaf, Bangladesh · November 2023 – April 2025

    Context

    WASH improvements in healthcare facilities need practical maintenance arrangements, realistic recurring budgets, and coordination between facility staff and local institutions.

    My contribution

    As Advocacy & IPC Supervisor, I worked on WASH FIT implementation, operation and maintenance planning, and life-cycle cost analysis for healthcare facilities, alongside coordination with health authorities and local government.

    Methods and activities

    • WASH FIT assessments and improvement planning.
    • Life-cycle cost analysis covering 17 healthcare facilities.
    • Operation and maintenance roadmaps.
    • Rainwater harvesting and environmental assessment inputs.

    Research connection

    How can life-cycle cost evidence and local maintenance arrangements support sustained WASH performance in healthcare facilities?

    Facility-level evidence, costing figures, and shareable documents remain to be selected and verified.
