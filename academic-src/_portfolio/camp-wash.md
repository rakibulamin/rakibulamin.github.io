**Caritas Bangladesh · Camps 4 and 17, Cox’s Bazar**

## Context

Refugee camp WASH services require ongoing maintenance, water-quality checks, and coordination to remain reliable through everyday operational pressures and emergencies.

## My contribution

As Technical Coordinator — WASH, my responsibilities cover technical oversight of water supply and sanitation services, water-quality monitoring, team coordination, and emergency response. I also undertake WASH Camp Focal Point responsibilities in Camp 17.

## Methods and activities

- Water supply operation and maintenance, including piped networks and tubewells.
- Chlorination and water-quality monitoring.
- Coordination with camp authorities and operational partners.
- Technical inputs to monitoring and donor reporting.

## Research connection

How can operational and water-quality data be used to identify service risks and prioritise preventive maintenance?
