## Research focus

Evaluation of water quality in the Shari–Goyain River using physicochemical parameters and a modified water quality index.

## My contribution

My research experience included literature review, geographic surveys, sample collection and testing, maintaining data records, analysis, and graphical presentation of water-quality conditions.

## Approach

- Field sampling and physicochemical water-quality testing.
- Analysis and graphical interpretation of water-quality parameters.
- Assessment using a modified water quality index.
