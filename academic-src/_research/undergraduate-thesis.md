**Undergraduate thesis · BSc in Civil & Environmental Engineering · SUST, 2018**

## Study overview

The undergraduate thesis examined high-rise building configurations under wind and earthquake loading using ETABS.

## Analytical foundation

This work forms part of my engineering foundation in computational modelling, interpretation of structural response, and comparison of design configurations.

## Methods

- ETABS structural modelling.
- Comparison of high-rise building shapes.
- Wind and earthquake loading.
