I am **Rakibul Amin**, a civil and environmental engineer working in humanitarian water, sanitation, and hygiene (WASH) in Cox’s Bazar, Bangladesh. I currently serve as **Technical Coordinator — WASH at Caritas Bangladesh**, supporting essential services in Rohingya refugee camps.

My interests lie in **drinking-water quality, resilient water and sanitation services, and geospatial approaches to environmental risks**. Through work in refugee camps and healthcare facilities, I have seen how engineering decisions, maintenance arrangements, and everyday water-use practices influence service reliability and public health. I am interested in bringing this experience into graduate research in water and environmental engineering.

## Research interests

- **Water quality and public health:** physicochemical assessment, household water safety, and exposure pathways.
- **Resilient WASH services:** operation and maintenance, life-cycle costs, and infrastructure performance in resource-constrained settings.
- **Geospatial analysis:** spatial approaches to water access, environmental vulnerability, and basin-scale water challenges.

## Selected research

My research experience includes physicochemical water-quality analysis of the **Shari–Goyain River**, using a modified water quality index. This work is associated with a conference paper in **ICERIE 2019**. I am also developing the documentation for my **Ganges–Brahmaputra–Meghna (GBM) basin work**.
