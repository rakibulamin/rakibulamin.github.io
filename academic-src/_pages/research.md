## Directions for graduate research

**Water quality and public health.** I am interested in the relationship between source quality, household storage, and water-use practices, particularly in settings with operational and resource constraints.

**Resilient water and sanitation services.** My professional experience motivates questions about preventive maintenance, life-cycle planning, and the long-term reliability of services in humanitarian settings and healthcare facilities.

**Geospatial approaches to water risks.** I want to strengthen the use of GIS and spatial data to examine environmental vulnerability and inform infrastructure priorities.
