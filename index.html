ABOUT ME

Engineering grounded in field experience.

Current positionTechnical Coordinator — WASHCaritas Bangladesh · May 2025–present

I currently serve as Technical Coordinator — WASH at Caritas Bangladesh, supporting essential services in Rohingya refugee camps in Cox’s Bazar. My work connects water supply, sanitation, and public health in settings where reliable services matter every day.

I am interested in bringing this experience into graduate research in water and environmental engineering. Through work in refugee camps and healthcare facilities, I have seen how engineering decisions, maintenance arrangements, and everyday water-use practices influence service reliability and public health.

Previously: Terre des hommes · HEKS/EPER · Action Against Hunger
